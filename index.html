The Altair 8800 - inspired a generation

The Smithsonian Museum describes the as the machine that “inaugurated the personal computer age.”

After appearing on the cover of *Popular Electronics* in January 1975, demand exploded.
Ed Roberts had hoped to sell about 200 units, within months MITS had a backlog of 4,000 orders.

The success didn't just start the personal-computer revolution,
it helped spawn computer clubs, stores, magazines and an ecosystem of add-on hardware. And inspired a generation:

  • MITS hired Bill Gates and Paul Allen to write the Altair BASIC interpreter. They founded Microsoft so they could send an invoice.
  • Steve Wozniak was inspired to design the Apple-I, and together with Steve Jobs founded Apple in 1976
  • Don French bought an and convinced his employer Tandy/Radio Shack to develop and sell its own computer.
    The TRS-80 Model I launched in 1977, sold 250.000+ units, becoming the best-selling personal computer of the 70s.

native HTML Web Components - available since 2016

The working simulator is created with a single <altair-8800> Custom Element HTML tag (aka Web Component)
The 2 main components are <display-bit-led> for the LED lamps and <toggle-switch> for the switches.
